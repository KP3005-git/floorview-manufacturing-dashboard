## Project description
A C# WinForms portfolio demonstration that turns sample production records into a clear manufacturing dashboard. It includes date and shift filters, completed-unit counts, first-pass yield, goal progress, failure breakdowns, hourly output, and CSV export. After-midnight records retain their production date. Includes a separate SQL Server reporting example. Uses synthetic data; no employer data or live factory connection.

## How to discuss it with a client
Explain that this is a demonstration prepared with AI assistance and synthetic
data, based on your experience with internal manufacturing tools. Describe the
date/shift filter and first-pass calculations, then discuss how you would adapt
the design to their actual data and workflow. Do not present it as a paid client
project or a deployed system.
